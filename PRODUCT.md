# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Altorancho's ecommerce/web team (Joaquín and colleagues), viewed on personal
notebooks (13"-15", 1366px+ wide) during the weekly sales numbers meeting.
Read-only reporting: no data entry, just scanning KPIs, charts and tables
quickly during a live discussion.

## Product Purpose
Replace the manual process of screenshotting Tienda Nube's native stats
panel. Surfaces sales KPIs, daily/channel revenue, top products, category and
payment breakdowns for Consolidado + each sales channel (Ecommerce, Lomas,
Belgrano, Alcorta, Mayorista). Success = the team can read the week's numbers
at a glance on a notebook screen without horizontal scrolling, overlapping
cards, or clipped values.

## Brand Personality
Functional, legible, fast to scan. Internal ops tool, not a marketing
surface — clarity and information density win over decorative polish.

## Anti-references
None specified — no particular look to avoid, just avoid layouts that break
(overflowing cards, cramped KPI numbers, illegible chart labels) at notebook
widths.

## Design Principles
- Fluid, not fixed-breakpoint: layouts must hold from ~1366px wide upward,
  not target one exact resolution.
- Numbers first: KPI values and chart labels must never be cut off, wrapped
  awkwardly, or shrunk unreadably to fit a card.
- Scan speed over decoration: this is read during a live meeting; density and
  legibility beat visual flourish.
- Reuse the existing token system (`tokens.css`) rather than introducing new
  colors/spacing scales.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific WCAG target stated. Keep existing contrast/legibility; don't
regress readability while tightening layouts for smaller screens.
